Know Your Rights

In the streets and in your home, knowledge is the only armor that never breaks. Here is the full Bill of Rights, translated for the real world:

The First Amendment

The Law: Freedom of religion, speech, press, assembly, and petition.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: You have the right to record police interactions in public. Your camera is your witness; keep it rolling as long as you aren't physically interfering.

The Second Amendment

The Law: The right to keep and bear arms.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: You have the right to protect yourself. Know your local carry laws and never volunteer information about your legal protection unless legally required.

The Third Amendment

The Law: Protection from quartering of troops.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: Your home is your castle. Government agents cannot occupy your property or force you to house them without your consent.

The Fourth Amendment

The Law: Protection against unreasonable searches and seizures.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: This is your primary shield. If they don't have a warrant, the magic words are always: "I do not consent to any searches." Force them to follow the law.

The Fifth Amendment

The Law: Right to due process, protection against self-incrimination and double jeopardy.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: You cannot talk your way out of an arrest, but you can talk your way into a conviction. Invoke your right immediately: "I am exercising my 5th Amendment right to remain silent."

The Sixth Amendment

The Law: Right to a speedy and public trial, impartial jury, and right to counsel.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: Never face the system alone. If questioned, simply say: "I want my lawyer." Once you say those exact words, they must stop questioning you.

The Seventh Amendment

The Law: Right to a trial by jury in civil cases.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: Even in non-criminal disputes over property or money, the system cannot just rubber-stamp a judgment behind closed doors. You have the right to present your case to a jury of your peers.

The Eighth Amendment

The Law: Protection against excessive bail, excessive fines, and cruel and unusual punishment.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: The punishment must fit the crime. The state cannot use impossible bail amounts simply to keep you locked up and break your spirit before a conviction.

The Ninth Amendment

The Law: Rights retained by the people.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: Just because a right isn't explicitly written on this list doesn't mean you don't have it. Your freedom extends far beyond what is printed on paper.

The Tenth Amendment

The Law: Powers delegated to the states and the people.

The Stay Dangerous Breakdown: The federal government only has the power explicitly given to it. Everything else belongs to the states and, ultimately, directly to you.
