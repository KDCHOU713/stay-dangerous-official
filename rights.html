Know Your Rights

In the streets and in your home, knowledge is the only armor that never breaks. Here are the pillars of your protection:

The First Amendment

The Law: Freedom of speech, press, and assembly.

The Breakdown: You have the right to record police interactions in public. Your camera is your witness; keep it rolling as long as you aren't physically interfering.

The Second Amendment

The Law: The right to keep and bear arms.

The Breakdown: You have the right to protect yourself. Know your local carry laws and never volunteer information about your legal protection unless legally required.

The Fourth Amendment

The Law: Protection against unreasonable searches and seizures.

The Breakdown: This is your shield. If they don't have a warrant, the magic words are: "I do not consent to any searches." Force them to follow the law.

The Fifth Amendment

The Law: Protection against self-incrimination.

The Breakdown: You cannot talk your way out of an arrest, but you can talk your way into a conviction. Invoke your right: "I am exercising my right to remain silent."

The Sixth Amendment

The Law: The right to a lawyer and a fair trial.

The Breakdown: Never face the system alone. If questioned, simply say: "I want my lawyer." Once you say that, they must stop questioning you.

The Eighth Amendment

The Law: Protection against excessive bail and cruel punishment.

The Breakdown: The punishment must fit the crime. This prevents the state from using impossible bails to keep you locked up without a conviction.
