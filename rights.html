Know Your Rights

In the streets and in your home, knowledge is the only armor that never breaks.

The First Amendment

The Law: Freedom of speech and assembly.

The Breakdown: You have the right to record the police in public spaces. Your camera is your witness.

The Fourth Amendment

The Law: Protection against unreasonable searches.

The Breakdown: If they don't have a warrant: "I do not consent to any searches."

The Fifth Amendment

The Law: Right to remain silent.

The Breakdown: Invoke it. "I am exercising my 5th Amendment right and I want my lawyer." Then, stop talking.
